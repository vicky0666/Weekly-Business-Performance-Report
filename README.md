
# Weekly Business Performance Report

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Superstore sales data and preparing a weekly business performance report for business owners.

The objective is to monitor weekly sales, profit, orders, and quantity, identify performance changes, and provide actionable business recommendations.

## Business Problem
Business owners often have sales data but limited time to analyze dashboards. This project aims to convert raw sales data into simple weekly insights that support better business decisions.

## Tools & Technologies
- MySQL – Data cleaning, SQL queries, weekly aggregations
- Power BI – Data visualization and business performance dashboard
- Excel – Data validation and analysis

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | $2.27M |
| Total Profit | $282.86K |
| Total Orders | ~5K |
| Total Quantity | ~37K |
| Profit Margin | 12.46% |


## Project Workflow

1. **Data Source:** Superstore sales dataset.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Cleaned and validated data using SQL.
3. **SQL Analysis:** Created daily and weekly sales analysis and a weekly reporting view.
4. **Power BI Dashboard:** Visualized sales, profit, orders, quantity and weekly trends.
5. **Business Insights:** Identified sales fluctuations and year-over-year changes.
6. **Reporting:** Prepared an executive business summary in PDF format.


## Analysis Performed
- Data cleaning and validation
- Daily and weekly sales analysis
- Weekly sales and profit comparisons
- Business performance trend analysis
- Identification of unusual sales movements
- Business recommendations

## Key Findings
- Weekly sales performance shows a fluctuating trend.
- December 2014 sales were approximately 17% lower than December 2013.
- Overall profit margin was approximately 12.46%.

## Business Recommendations
- Investigate the reasons behind declining sales.
- Monitor weekly sales fluctuations.
- Review product pricing and discount strategies.
- Focus on improving profitability.
